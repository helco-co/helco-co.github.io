The HELCO Story

A Legacy of Service Excellence and Client Satisfaction.

HANY EL ARABY is an Egyptian independent firm established in 2005 of chartered and certified accountants and registered auditors. We provide comprehensive audit, accounting, tax, and financial advisory services to a wide range of enterprises and companies.

Our clients cover a wide array of industries, giving us unique opportunities for deep sector specialization. It is our long history of commitment to service excellence that has brought us to our current position as trusted enterprise advisers.

We are well known for our close involvement with our clients and their businesses. By working to understand their operations and committing to meet their complex needs, our partners and staff take an active role in helping clients succeed. We continually invest in superior technology, rigorous methodologies, and high-caliber professionals.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide our clients with high-quality audit and advisory services to assist them in keeping up with the rapidly changing risks facing their businesses, and to improve the quality, transparency, and effectiveness of their internal audit operations. We strive to be the standard of excellence in financial governance.

Code of Conduct

At HELCO, we are committed to doing business and serving our clients following the highest standards of ethical conduct and behavior. Our Code of Conduct applies to all firm personnel and governs all aspects of our business practices, behavior, and relationships inside and outside the firm.

It serves as our guide to doing the right thing, reinforcing our culture and commitment to ethics, integrity, and professional excellence in all that we do. We expect all personnel to maintain absolute independence and consult with others if the right course of action in a given situation is ever unclear.

Institutional Standards

HELCO Core Values

Our values (Collaboration, Leadership, Excellence, Agility, Respect, and Responsibility) are the most important references for who we are as a firm. They are the foundation of our institutional operating model and guide our approach to every engagement.

We Lead by Example

At every level of the firm, from new associates to partners, we act in a way that exemplifies the standard we expect of one another. Leadership at HELCO is not a title; it is a daily commitment to setting the example we ask our people and our clients to follow.

We Work Together

We bring out the best in each other by sharing knowledge freely across our audit, tax, and advisory teams. These strong, synergetic working relationships let us draw on the full breadth of the firm's expertise for every engagement, not just the specialists assigned to it.

We Respect the Individual

We respect people for who they are, and for the distinct knowledge, skills, and experience each one brings to the team. That respect shapes how we hire, mentor, and promote, so that every voice at HELCO is heard on its merits.

Seek Facts & Provide Insight

We challenge assumptions and pursue the facts wherever they lead, even when that means delivering an answer a client did not expect. This rigor is what has built our reputation as trusted, objective advisers over two decades of practice.

Open & Honest Communication

We share information frequently and constructively, both within our teams and with the clients we serve. When situations are difficult, we address them directly, with the courage and candor our clients rely on to make informed decisions.

Committed to Communities

We act as responsible corporate citizens in every market we operate in, from Cairo to Dubai and Riyadh. Community engagement broadens our people's skills and perspective, and reflects our belief that a firm's success is tied to the health of the communities it serves.

Above All, Integrity

Integrity underpins every other value we hold. We constantly strive to uphold the highest professional standards, provide sound and honest advice, and rigorously maintain the independence our clients depend on.
